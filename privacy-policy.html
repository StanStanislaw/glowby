4. Advertising & Tracking Consent

In the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we use an in-app consent screen to collect your preference for analytics and personalized advertising before any non-essential tracking begins. On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework provides additional control over cross-app tracking.

If you decline personalized advertising or deny ATT permission, we will serve non-personalized ads and will not access your device's advertising identifier. Our ad partners may use non-identifying contextual signals for ad delivery and fraud prevention, but will not use device identifiers for behavioral targeting. You can change your ATT preference at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Important: Glowby is a mixed-audience game rated 4+. We use a neutral age screen on first launch to determine age-appropriate experiences.

If a user indicates they are under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in their jurisdiction), we automatically:

  • Disable Firebase Analytics and all non-essential tracking
  • Disable personalized advertising — only contextual, child-directed ads are shown
  • Do not request tracking consent (ATT prompt is not shown)
  • Configure AdMob with child-directed treatment flags and G content rating
  • Disable custom username creation — under-age users are assigned an auto-generated player name to prevent accidental sharing of personal information

We do not require children to provide any personal information to use the game. Because child profiles are entirely anonymous and store no personal identifiers on our servers, data must be managed directly on the child's device using the in-app "Delete Account" feature or by uninstalling the app. Parents may contact us at Loading... with any questions about our child safety practices.

7. Data Retention

  • Game Progress: Stored locally on your device and in iCloud until you delete the app or your data
  • Leaderboard Data: Custom usernames and Firestore leaderboard data are retained while your account is active, and deleted upon request or after 2 years of inactivity. Scores submitted to Apple Game Center are retained by Apple and must be managed in your iOS device Settings.
  • Support Requests: Retained for 2 years for quality assurance
  • Analytics: Pseudonymous user-level event data is retained in Firebase for up to 14 months, after which only fully anonymized, aggregated statistics are kept.
